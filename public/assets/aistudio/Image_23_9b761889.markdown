## Brand & Style

The brand conveys advanced medical precision, reassurance, and modern clinical intelligence. Designed for tech-forward patients navigating complex healthcare ecosystems, the interface substitutes cold, clinical sterility with a serene, nocturnal sanctuary. Every screen prioritizes diagnostic clarity, calm confidence, and immediate access to care.

The aesthetic fuses **Futuristic Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**:
- Ultra-deep obsidian and charcoal navy substrates isolate focus and alleviate visual fatigue.
- Luminous bioluminescent cyan and teal accents serve as navigational beacons and status cues.
- Subtle frosted transradiancies, sub-pixel borders, and subtle glowing pulse indicators infuse vitality into static clinical data.
- Generous touch targets engineered specifically for one-handed mobile ergonomics ensure stress-free triage and appointment flows.

## Colors

The palette relies on a carefully calibrated range of dark surfaces paired with photonic accents:

- **Primary Accent (`#06B6D4`)**: Electric Cyan. Directs the primary visual narrative; used for active tab indicators, key interactive actions, and forward motion.
- **Secondary Accent (`#14B8A6` & `#0EA5E9`)**: High-performance Teal and Sky Blue. Applied to filters, secondary CTA micro-interactions, category chips, and analytical health metrics.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Emerald Green. Dedicated strictly to affirmative status indicators: real-time availability, verified credentials, confirmed appointments, and telemedicine connectivity.
- **Neutral Bases (`#0B0F19` & `#111827`)**: Canvas Background and Elevated Charcoal Layer. A rich, blue-tinted void that absorbs glare and amplifies content legibility.
- **Text & Foreground Hierarchy**:
  - Headings: Crisp Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for instantaneous optical hierarchy.
  - Body & Form Text: Soft Muted Slate (`#CBD5E1`) for effortless sustained reading.
  - Subtext & Captions: Faded Slate (`#94A3B8` / `#64748B`) for auxiliary clinical details and metadata.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is used across all typographic roles. Its geometric foundation coupled with soft humanist terminals produces a contemporary, clinical feel without appearing cold or machine-like.

- **Headlines**: Set in bold and semi-bold weights with negative letter tracking to preserve tightness against deep dark canvas backgrounds.
- **Body**: Uses regular weight (`400`) at comfortable line heights (1.4–1.5x) to maintain effortless legibility through dense doctor biographies, treatment tags, and schedules.
- **Labels & Micro-data**: Rendered in medium to bold weights (`600`/`700`) with expanded tracking for all-caps badges, verification markers, and dynamic schedule tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around mobile-first touch ergonomics:

- **Grid Strategy**: Fluid 4-column structure on mobile devices transitioning to an 8-column layout on small tablets. Layouts snap to a strict 4px/8px modular spacing rhythm.
- **Thumb Zone Optimization**: Primary navigations, urgent action sheets, and core booking flows anchor within the bottom 45% of the viewport. Top areas are reserved for search triggers, contextual badges, and status feedback.
- **Margins & Gutters**: Standard screen edge margin is `1rem` on mobile, scaling to `1.5rem` on larger displays. Inter-card gap is standardized to `space-md` (`1rem`) to keep grouped metadata readable and distinct.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses luminous glassmorphic layering and diffuse photonic glows rather than heavy structural shadows:

- **Surface 0 (Canvas)**: `#0B0F19` base substrate.
- **Surface 1 (Base Cards & Bottom Sheets)**: `#111827` at 85% opacity with a `16px` backdrop-blur filter and an ultra-subtle border stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Surface 2 (Interactive Floating Modules & Modals)**: `#1E293B` at 70% opacity, overlaid with a top-lit inner gradient border (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)` fading to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`).
- **Photonic Glow**: Floating interactive states and active status dots emit a soft, diffused ambient aura (`0 0 16px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)` for cyan, `0 0 14px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)` for live availability).

## Shapes

The interface relies on smooth, expansive curves to soften clinical interactions:

- **Cards & Primary Modules**: Styled with `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / 16px) corner radiuses to create organic, friendly framing for doctor profiles and service lists.
- **Inputs & Search Trays**: Styled with `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px) to balance structured input with touch ergonomics.
- **Badges, Filters & Status Pills**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to create an immediate visual contrast against geometric card layouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book/Consult)**: Cyan-to-teal linear gradient fill (`from-[#06B6D4] to-[#14B8A6]`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-xl`, with a subtle neon floor glow on hover/press. Minimum touch target height of 48px.
- **Secondary (Profile/Chat)**: Translucent slate background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), muted cyan text on active tap.
- **Ghost/Icon Button**: Pure transparent surface with 44x44px ergonomic touch bounds; icons tinted `#94A3B8`, brightening to `#FFFFFF` when engaged.

### Doctor Discovery Cards
- Enclosed in `rounded-2xl` containers utilizing the Surface 1 glass treatment.
- Top-right corner hosts an embedded Emerald status indicator paired with a pulsating halo when the physician is actively on call.
- Integrated doctor avatar (64x64px, `rounded-xl`) framed by a micro-border of `#0EA5E9` at 30% opacity.
- Embedded star ratings incorporate amber-gold stars (`#FBBF24`) coupled with crisp numeric tallies in `#CBD5E1`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) tags displaying medical specializations (e.g., "Cardiology", "Neurology", "Pediatrics").
- **Inactive**: `#111827` surface with `#94A3B8` label and hairline neutral border.
- **Selected**: Semi-translucent `#06B6D4` background (15% opacity), vibrant cyan border (`#06B6D4`), and pure white text.

### Inputs & Search
- Recessed slate background (`#0F172A`) featuring an inset search icon in `#64748B`.
- Active focus state triggers an outer glow of `0 0 0 2px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)` and shifts the border stroke to solid `#06B6D4`.
- Integrated voice search and specialty quick-clear triggers placed within accessible thumb reach on mobile.

### Badges & Pulsing Indicators
- **Verified Doctor Pill**: Micro-badge featuring an emerald checkmark shield on a soft emerald tint (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`).
- **Pulse Beacon**: 8px green dot featuring a dual-ring CSS animation expanding outward to simulate a cardiac rhythm, signifying immediate video-call availability.
